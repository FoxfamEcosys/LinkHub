# Product

## Register
Brand: Veri's personal VTuber website, with a supporting owner editor.

## Users and purpose
Visitors discover Veri, watch streams, join the community, read rules, find links and credits, shop, and catch up on updates. Veri manages published content directly on the website.

## Brand personality
Playful, personal, welcoming. Preserve the voice from the existing homepage and the approved first visual concept.

## Anti-references
No shrine-garden redesign. No generated replacement for the supplied splash artwork. No generic business dashboard. No changes to approved composition beyond requested additions.

## Design principles
- Faithfully preserve option 1: charcoal, periwinkle, bold left heading and original artwork on the right.
- Main site uses the explicitly requested PostHog central window and outside navigation.
- Keep stream rules and merch prominent.
- Preserve source artwork; use only display sizing/cropping.
- Keyboard access, visible focus, mobile layouts, reduced motion, readable contrast.
